## 1. Project Identification
- **Project Name:** LifeProgreX
- **Project Manager:** Satya Pranav Nagunoori
- **Project Sponsor:** Clark University - Department of Computer Science
- **Draft Date:** April 27, 2026

## 2. Executive Summary
**LifeProgreX** is a mobile application designed to revolutionize how individuals track personal growth. By integrating health data, habit tracking, and generative AI, the project aims to provide a "Life Resume" that goes beyond traditional metrics to reflect true holistic progress.

## 3. Business Case / Purpose
Many health and productivity apps are siloed, focusing on only one aspect of life. LifeProgreX addresses this by providing a unified platform where physical activity, mental habits, and AI-driven insights converge to help users achieve a balanced and productive lifestyle.

## 4. SMART Objectives
- **Specific:** Deliver a cross-platform Flutter app with Firebase backend and Gemini AI integration.
- **Measurable:** Achieve a minimum of 95% data accuracy in HealthKit synchronization.
- **Achievable:** Build core modules (Analytics, AI Coach, Rewards) within the 15-week Capstone timeframe.
- **Relevant:** Direct alignment with the university's project management and software engineering standards.
- **Time-bound:** Complete final deployment and presentation by the end of the Spring 2026 semester.

## 5. Project Scope

### **In-Scope**
- User Authentication (Google & Email)
- Health Data Integration (HealthKit/Google Fit)
- AI Coach Interface with Gemini SDK
- Interactive Progress Dashboards
- Local and Push Notification Systems

### **Out-of-Scope**
- Social networking features between users.
- Desktop or web-only application versions.
- Integration with tertiary wearable devices (e.g., specific high-end medical monitors).
